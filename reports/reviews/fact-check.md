# Fact-check: citations, quotes and paper-attributed numbers in the site entries

Date: 2026-11-28. Scope: the 33 files in `site/src/content/entries/*.md`: the citation metadata, the `quote` fields,
the numbers and findings the body text attributes to published work, and the `replications` written by other people.
Numbers written as `{{templates}}` are the atlas's own results and are out of scope. So are the `outcome: ours` rows,
apart from the parts of their text that describe a paper.

## Method

- **arXiv:** title, authors, dates, comments and licence were read from `export.arxiv.org/abs/<id>` for all 35 arXiv
  IDs cited. Full text was searched in the arXiv HTML (2026 papers) or in the PDF converted with `pdftotext` (older
  papers).
- **transformer-circuits.pub, goodfire.com and distill.pub:** the raw HTML was downloaded, converted to text and
  searched with regular expressions for each claimed number. WebFetch's summaries were not relied on for numbers.
- **GitHub:** repository, licence and README were read through the GitHub API, plus `FINDINGS.md` where the claim
  depends on it.
- **LessWrong, Hugging Face and blogs:** read with WebFetch. Exact numbers were re-checked with curl where possible.
- **Link check:** all 132 unique URLs in the 33 entries were requested with curl, following redirects. 131 return
  HTTP 200. `copy-suppression.streamlit.app` redirects (303) to a Streamlit login page.
- **Wording of evidence:** the "source says" column paraphrases the source and gives its section, table or figure, so
  each point can be checked there. Numbers are copied exactly.

Overall, every arXiv ID, transformer-circuits page, Goodfire page and repository cited exists and matches its title and
first authors. All the headline numbers attributed to the Anthropic, Goodfire and academic papers matched the source
(list at the end). The problems fall into three groups:

- **Overstated "replicated by others" rows (rows 1–5).** In each case the linked page reports partial agreement,
  disagreement, or a different cause.
- **One misattributed number (row 6).** The jlens entry attaches a 59% figure to the wrong experiment.
- **Quote sources (rows 7–10).** Three quotes are attributed to arXiv papers but appear only in the companion Goodfire
  blog posts, and one quote changes capitalisation.

Nothing found contradicts a central claim of any entry.

**Status (2026-11-28):** all 22 fixes below were applied to the entry files; rows 1 and 5 use the suggested wording,
row 7 keeps the quote but attributes it to the blog and marks it as said of vision models, row 14 states that the atlas's
own isometry metric differs from the paper's.

   (HTTP 303) to a Streamlit login page, so the app's content could not be checked. The paper's abstract does link this

   LessWrong comment by Nanda on the essay's linkpost. The secondary accounts agree with the site's summary: superposition
   is still real, and weak activations are not addressed. The comment itself was not read in full; no URL is given on the
   site.

   include Figure 18's rows.
4. **The model behind the Neuronpedia addition graphs** (row 5). The Landscape post does not name it.
